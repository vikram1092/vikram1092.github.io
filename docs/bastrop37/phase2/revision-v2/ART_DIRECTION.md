# Delivery revision 2 — art direction

Status: work in progress for user visual review. The expanded v1 package was rejected for theme/art quality; passing layout checks did not make it visually accepted. This revision changes one Delivery treatment only. The original `phase2/` package and source art remain preserved reference.

## What must improve

The original `scarlet-bike-neon-traffic.jpg` establishes a strong scarlet silhouette, deep city canyon, wet reflected light, substantial foreground scale, and detailed inked/painted machinery. The rejected `full-1440x900-service.png` loses that identity through flat SVG blocks, ungrounded front-elevation props, broad gray shoulders and repeated interface rectangles. Revision 2 must restore coherent scale, material, atmosphere and authored hierarchy, rather than add superficial borders or more widgets.

## Agreed scene contract

Rear-chase 2D/faked-depth composition. Desktop vanishing point 50% width, horizon 42% scene height. Wet road fills the foreground; the clear aperture widens from approximately x43–57% at the horizon to x14–86% at the bottom. Layer detailed neighborhood/shop/service-frontage flanks in front of the existing distant skyline. Keep the future traversable surface and hazards separate from artwork. No baked character, readable generated wording or coast.

Charcoal blue ambient light, rain-dark concrete, oxidized steel, chipped dirty-ivory civic paint and a few amber utility pools establish the scene. Cyan remains technical/local communication, not decorative neon on every surface. Scarlet Jo remains the first color read. Street wear belongs on physical surfaces; text and body edges stay crisp.

New raster candidates: `delivery-architecture-v2.png`, `signal-dead-v2.png`, `service-gate-open-v2.png`. The signal needs a distinctive heavy housing, deep hoods, conduit and unlit lenses. The open gate needs convincing structural depth and an asymmetric service silhouette, blank sign surfaces and clear opening. Neither can read as a flat gray box. Existing portrait and Omega identity are reused.

Rider body approximately17% desktop width, capped near240px, grounded at92% scene height. Mobile uses a larger relative rider but raises it for reading; architecture is cropped/repositioned at original proportions to align its horizon, not squeezed into thin towers. Final crop and collision implications remain Phase 3 work.

## HUD contract

Bike-associated instruments remain screen overlays, never a first-person cockpit. Open angular rails, segmented energy and strong condensed speed establish an authored instrument silhouette. Limit simultaneous readouts to what the current Delivery moment needs. Communication uses a distinct asymmetric municipal strip with approved neutral portrait, or local Omega accent. Body text stays clear and generous; avoid repeated glass-card dashboard boxes.

ACTION should read road gap → rider → objective. STORY should read speaker/message → advance affordance, with rider and safe road retained. Use approved L1 copy. Show full sentences; no automatic dismissal. Action may show the service route after Omega's advice; during the preceding neutral dialogue stretch keep the next gate beyond view. Only Jo, Vlad and Omega speak; no early betrayal cue.

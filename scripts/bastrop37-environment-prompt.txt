CITY SKYLINE LAYER

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 background-only skyline layer for a real-time arcade motorcycle game
Primary request: Create an awe-inspiring futuristic midnight megacity background for a high rear-chase motorcycle camera. This is the city layer only; a separate moving roadway will be composited over its lower portion.
Input images: scarlet-bike-neon-traffic.jpg is the approved BASTROP37 world concept and primary art-direction reference. city-expressway-v2.jpg is a reference for architectural detail, atmosphere, material quality, and palette, but its low road-level camera angle must not be repeated.
Scene/backdrop: Immense vertical city canyon in rain, needle towers disappearing into storm clouds, layered bridges and elevated transit rings, architectural ribs, distant fog shelves, tiny warm windows, selective scarlet and cyan light, believable depth and monumental scale.
Composition/framing: Exact wide 16:9 landscape; camera positioned several meters above the roadway as if directly behind and above a motorcycle, looking downward about 12–15 degrees; centered vanishing point near 32% from the top; tall detailed architecture concentrated on both side thirds and across the far horizon; center remains readable; lower 55% should be unobstructed dark atmospheric space that can be covered by a separate perspective road layer; no foreground road surface, close guardrails, or foreground objects.
Style/medium: Premium cinematic sci-fi concept art, crisp highly detailed hard-surface architecture, painterly atmospheric depth, polished game key-art finish, realistic material detail; not pixel art, low-poly, or flat vector art.
Lighting/mood: Awe-inspiring, immense, dangerous and beautiful; deep navy night, steel-blue rain, crimson and restrained cyan accents; mist and volumetric depth.
Constraints: Environment only; no road surface in foreground, motorcycle, rider, cars, drones, people, HUD, logos, legible text, numbers, or watermark; do not place any object in the central gameplay corridor.
Avoid: Low camera at asphalt level, blocky silhouettes, simplistic rectangles, low-detail facades, voxel art, flat gradients, neon clutter, magenta wash, mirrored symmetry.

LOOPING ROAD LAYER

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertically looping roadway texture for a real-time arcade racing game
Primary request: Create a premium wet futuristic five-lane expressway surface asset viewed from a perfectly orthographic top-down camera. It will be perspective-warped and scrolled beneath a high rear-chase camera, so the source itself must have zero perspective convergence.
Input images: scarlet-bike-neon-traffic.jpg is the approved reference for wet asphalt, material richness, rain, reflection color, lane language, and finish. city-expressway-v2.jpg is a reference for road-surface quality only.
Surface: Dark nearly black rain-soaked asphalt with fine aggregate, drainage seams, subtle repaired cracks, shallow puddling, delicate tire wear, and richly detailed but restrained elongated scarlet/cyan/amber reflected light. Five equal-width driving lanes running vertically from top edge to bottom edge. Four parallel dashed off-white lane dividers plus subtle outer edge strips. No objects.
Composition/framing: Square texture tile; perfectly straight-on top-down orthographic view; road fills the entire canvas edge to edge; all lane dividers remain parallel and vertical with equal spacing; no horizon, skyline, curb, barrier, shoulder, or vanishing point. Make the top and bottom edges visually compatible for vertical looping; keep the left/right edges dark.
Style/medium: High-end cinematic sci-fi game texture, photorealistic concept-art finish, crisp micro-detail, physically believable wet asphalt and reflections, matching BASTROP37.
Lighting/mood: Midnight rain; deep navy-black pavement; restrained scarlet and cyan reflections; high contrast but gameplay-readable.
Constraints: Exactly five lanes and four dashed internal divider lines; no letters, numbers, arrows, logos, watermark, vehicles, people, debris, buildings, horizon, perspective, solid center line, or duplicated competing markings.
Avoid: Low-poly geometry, chunky slabs, checkerboard tiles, broad opaque color bands, close-up macro pebbles, strong diagonal light streaks, perspective convergence, road-level camera, fixed vehicle shadows.

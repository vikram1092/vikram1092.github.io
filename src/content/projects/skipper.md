Skipper began with a familiar engineering problem: the conversation containing the real context happens in Slack, while the durable work has to live somewhere else. I built and launched an AI workflow that bridges that gap for technical teams.

The work spans product discovery, engineering, positioning, customer outreach, and the repeated small launches required to turn a useful idea into something people pay for and use every day.
